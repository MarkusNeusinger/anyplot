# Political plots use invented parties and seats

- Date: 2026-10-04
- Proposed: the parliament chart's implementations mixed real and real-sounding parties. One drew
  the real European Parliament groups, and its review scored that 5 of 5 as "authentic political
  data"; three others swapped the parties for a faculty senate, a board or sports conferences to
  dodge the review's rule on politics.
- Decided: the spec uses no real data and no real parties, so that the catalogue is never read as
  taking a political side. The review's rule now excludes real politics only: a clearly fictional
  chamber is never marked down.
