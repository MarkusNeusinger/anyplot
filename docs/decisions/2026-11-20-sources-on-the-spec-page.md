# A spec's sources get a section of their own on its page, with links you can click

- Date: 2026-11-20
- Proposed: on the same decision page as the two kinds of source, where the site shows a spec's
  sources once the spec names them.
- Decided: a "Sources" section of its own on the spec page, shown only on specs that have entries,
  so every other page stays as it is, and the same entries in what the site gives search engines and
  language models: the page's structured data and the machine-readable views of a spec. His answer
  on the decision page, with the condition that the links can be clicked.
