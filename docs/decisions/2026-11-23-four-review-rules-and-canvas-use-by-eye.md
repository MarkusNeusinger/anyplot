# Four review rules the judges applied, and canvas use judged by eye

- Date: 2026-11-23
- Proposed: In the blind before-and-after checks of the regeneration batches of 2026-11-20 to 2026-11-22, the judges deducted for things no review rule named: an axis far wider than its data, ticks a value cannot be read from, a color or marker without a key, and a label whose color ignores its fill. The layout score still rated canvas use in percentages, against the rule that review rules hold no hard thresholds. The generator prompts taught the opposite of the first rule, because their only axis-limit example started at zero. The options were to build the rules and the layout wording on one branch and test them in the joint review retest with the other rule changes, to test them now with a retest of their own, or not now.
- Decided: Build them on one branch and test them in the joint retest, by his rule of one proper test after all rule changes; the generator prompts' zero-start example says it is the bar baseline. He gave no reason of his own beyond that rule.
