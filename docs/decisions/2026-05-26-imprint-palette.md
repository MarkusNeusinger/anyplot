# Every plot uses Imprint, a colourblind-safe palette of the project's own

- Date: 2026-05-26
- Proposed: the plots used Okabe-Ito, and a palette called variant D had been adopted three days
  before (#7617). Four rounds followed. Two compared candidate palettes by colour distance under
  normal vision and three simulated colour-vision deficiencies; in the third, five reviewer agents
  picked the muted of two finalists; the fourth settled the order and the anchors.
- Decided: recorded on 2026-05-26. Imprint, eight hues and three semantic anchors, becomes the
  default of every plot. On the about page he writes: "it took four rounds: the colour-distance
  numbers had to agree with what I like to look at."
- Where: #7692, `app/src/pages/AboutPage.tsx`, `docs/reference/palette-variants/`
