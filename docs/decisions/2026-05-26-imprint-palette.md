# Every plot uses Imprint, a colourblind-safe palette of the project's own

- Date: 2026-05-26
- Proposed: the plots used Okabe-Ito. A first round of candidates, compared by colour distance under
  normal vision and three simulated colour-vision deficiencies, led to a palette called variant D,
  adopted on 2026-05-23 (#7617). Three more rounds followed: a second of candidates, a third in which
  five reviewer agents picked the muted of two finalists, and a fourth that settled the order and the
  anchors.
- Decided: recorded on 2026-05-26. Imprint, eight hues and three semantic anchors, becomes the
  default of every plot. On the about page he writes: "it took four rounds: the colour-distance
  numbers had to agree with what I like to look at."
- Where: #7692, #7617, `app/src/pages/AboutPage.tsx`, `docs/reference/palette-variants/`
