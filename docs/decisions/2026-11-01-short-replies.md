# The chat's replies are short, with a question only when it matters

- Date: 2026-11-01
- Proposed: the chat's replies as they stood at a user test on 2026-11-01.
- Decided: in his words, "Replies should be short and to the point, no waffle, and follow-up

  at most one question; the notices the data rules require stay.
