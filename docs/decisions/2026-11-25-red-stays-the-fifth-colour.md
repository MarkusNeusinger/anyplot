# Matte red stays the fifth categorical colour, and takes a category that means loss at any position

- Date: 2026-11-25
- Proposed: the style guide called the palette's red `#AE3030` a colour "reached intentionally, not
  by ordinal position", while the generator and the review's palette criterion list it fifth in
  the default order, so the joint retest of #14241 and #14243 showed the review deducting colour
  points from plots that followed the generator. Two ways out were offered: skip red in the
  default order and use it only with intent, or keep it fifth.
- Decided: red stays fifth. In his words, red is pushed back in the palette on purpose; with many
  series that carry no meaning it comes at its place after green, lavender, blue and ochre, and
  where something in the plot is bad, that category takes the red.
- Where: #14257
