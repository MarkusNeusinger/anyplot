# The generator checks its file's mandates and its plot's claims before it commits

- Date: 2026-11-26
- Proposed: The blind check of the regeneration batch of 2026-11-26 sorted the remarks on 260 pairs into 79 defect classes. Three of them pointed at the generator rather than at the review: lets-plot figures with a fixed aspect that the canvas wrap painted but left in the middle of blank bands (12 replaced files); library-prompt lines that a regeneration carried into about half of its files because no gate checked them (bokeh's page paint in 9 of 16 replaced files, equal pygal tick sizes in 7 of 16); and plots that contradict their own data or subject, which a review of the picture cannot recompute (sealed mazes, a "Mercator" title over another projection, change indicators that contradict their sparkline). The fixes on the table were a lets-plot canvas rule, per-library rules in the static contract check that the generator runs on its own file, and a computed check of the plot's own claims in the generator's self-check.
- Decided: He picked these three items from the batch check on 2026-11-26. The details were settled in the session that built them: which library-prompt lines become rules, the colour rule staying advisory, the claims sharing Step 4's one fix round, and the forms of the `Contract self-check:` and `Claims self-check:` lines.
