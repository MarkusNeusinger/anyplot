# Plausible for analytics, with a dashboard anyone can see

- Date: 2026-01-05
- Proposed: Google Analytics, which in his words rests on tracking each visitor personally.
- Decided: recorded by 2026-01-05: Plausible counts the site's visits and events, with no cookies for
  visitors. Its dashboard is public and linked from the README by 2026-04-14; the analytics reference
  adds that every event is therefore one a reader from outside understands. He confirmed it as his
  decision on 2026-11-19. His reason, given that day: he likes statistics, but he decided against
  personal tracking on purpose, even though some things would surely be easier to analyse with it;
  and it mattered to him that the dashboard is public, so anyone can see the stats.
