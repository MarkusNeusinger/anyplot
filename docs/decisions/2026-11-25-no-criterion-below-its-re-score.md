# A regeneration may not trade one review criterion for another

- Date: 2026-11-25
- Proposed: the regen gate let the total score fall by one point against the re-score of the live
  file, so a gain on one criterion could pay for a loss on another. A second opinion on the
  continuous-ramp change showed the case: the new ramp gains a VQ-07 point, and design may then
  fall by two while the regeneration still merges. On 2026-11-24 he chose a floor in its own pull
  request, after the joint retest; it was built with three variants for its reach: all 24
  criteria, the technical criteria only as on the adapt route, or all 24 with a reason the review
  may name.
- Decided: every one of the 24 criteria, design and library mastery included, must score at least
  its re-score, without exception other than a change request the review confirms applied;
  otherwise the live file stays. His rule that a regeneration only improves a pair and that a
  trade is a keep is the reason; that this floor follows from it is the agents' reading.
