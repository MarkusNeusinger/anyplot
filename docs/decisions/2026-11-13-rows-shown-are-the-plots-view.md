# Which rows a plot shows, and in what order, is the plot's; the table stays as pasted

- Date: 2026-11-13
- Proposed: a selection, a top N and a sort were steps of the table, so every tweak sent the user
  back to an earlier table version. In a persona round, "sort biggest first" on a finished plot
  became a sort of the table, a new data version and a second tap on the plot button.
- Decided: which rows the plot shows is a view of the plot, adjusted like axis limits after a look
  at the picture. The order of bars, categories, series or legend entries, and a figure such as a
  total in the title, are the plot's too. Cleaning stays a step of the table, and the user's own
  word wins.
