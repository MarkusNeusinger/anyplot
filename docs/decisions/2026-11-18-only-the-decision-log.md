# Of five ways to make his part visible, the owner takes one: this log, for what matters

- Date: 2026-11-18
- Proposed: five measures. A log of decisions; a fixed section in every pull request saying what he
  decided; review comments under his name for what he tested and sent back; reading the diffs where
  a mistake hurts; a list of what he chose not to build. The log's first draft held nine entries of
  two days, small matters among them.
- Decided: only the log, as light as it can be, and only for decisions that matter: they are written
  down so that good stories can be found later, and not every small thing belongs here. No ritual on
  GitHub, since he tells the agent in the session what he does not like, there and then. For diffs,
  the agent points him to one when it thinks his look is worth it.
