# Of five ways to make his part visible, the owner takes one: the decision log

- Date: 2026-11-18
- Proposed: five measures. A log of decisions; a fixed section in every pull request saying what he
  decided; review comments under his name for what he tested and sent back; reading the diffs where
  a mistake hurts; a list of what he chose not to build.
- Decided: only the log, and as light as it can be: decisions are written down so that good stories
  can be found later. No ritual on GitHub, since he tells the agent in the session what he does not
  like, there and then. For diffs, the agent points him to one when it thinks his look is worth it.
