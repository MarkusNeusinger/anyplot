# Of five ways to make his part visible, the owner takes one: this log

- Date: 2026-11-18
- Proposed: five measures. A log of decisions; a fixed section in every pull request saying what he
  decided; review comments under his name for what he tested and sent back; reading the diffs where
  a mistake hurts; a list of what he chose not to build. The log's first draft held nine entries of
  two days, small matters among them, and its second a bar by category (no wording, no number, no
  layout).
- Decided: only the log, as light as it can be. Decisions are written down so that good stories can
  be found later; the folder is not flooded with pointless things, and it may hold more than the
  highlights a README would list. An entry has a fixed form and names its source. No ritual on
  GitHub, since he tells the agent in the session what he does not like, there and then. For diffs,
  the agent points him to one when it thinks his look is worth it.
