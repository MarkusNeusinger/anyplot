# The project is open and free, without sign-ups

- Date: 2025-11-18
- Proposed: a public catalogue of plotting examples, and the terms it is offered on.
- Decided: the code has been under the MIT License since the first commit. He confirmed it as his
  decision on 2026-11-19. His reason, given that day: from the start it mattered to him that
  everything is open and free, and without sign-ups. Later terms: the about page says since
  2026-04-19 that issues, releases and discussions happen on GitHub, with no separate forum, signup
  or account, and the style guide since 2026-04-16 that the catalogue carries no ads and no affiliate
  links.
- Where: `README.md`, `app/src/pages/AboutPage.tsx`, `docs/reference/style-guide.md`
