# The project is open and free: MIT-licensed, on GitHub, with no sign-up and no ads

- Date: 2025-11-18
- Proposed: a public catalogue of plotting examples, and the terms it is offered on.
- Decided: the code has been under the MIT License since the first commit; everything happens on
  GitHub, with no separate forum, signup or account; and the catalogue carries no ads and no
  affiliate links. He confirmed it as his decision on 2026-11-19. His reason, given that day: from
  the start it mattered to him that everything is open and free, and without sign-ups.
- Where: `README.md`, `app/src/pages/AboutPage.tsx`, `docs/reference/style-guide.md`
