# pyplots.ai goes bigger and becomes anyplot.ai

- Date: 2026-04-23
- Proposed: pyplots.ai, a catalogue of Python plotting examples, after a few months online.
- Decided: in his words on the about page: "after a few months on pyplots.ai, I decided to go bigger.
  in April 2026 it became anyplot.ai." The style guide's origin story, told in his voice, gives the
  reason: "It grew when I realized people wanted the same examples across different libraries, and
  the same safe palette everywhere." The relaunch, release 2.0.0 of 2026-04-23, moved the domain,
  made the spec URL `/{specId}/{language}/{library}`, gave the schema a table of languages, and gave
  every plot a light and a dark render.
- Where: #5289, `app/src/pages/AboutPage.tsx`, `docs/reference/style-guide.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`
