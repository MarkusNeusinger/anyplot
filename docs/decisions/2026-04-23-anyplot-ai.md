# pyplots.ai goes bigger and becomes anyplot.ai

- Date: 2026-04-23
- Proposed: pyplots.ai, a catalogue of Python plotting examples, after a few months online.
- Decided: in his words on the about page: "after a few months on pyplots.ai, I decided to go bigger.
  in April 2026 it became anyplot.ai." The same paragraph gives the reason: colleagues, and later
  people who found it online, wanted ideas for which plot fits their data, and to get to know
  libraries they hadn't used yet. The relaunch, release 2.0.0 of 2026-04-23, moved the domain, made
  the spec URL `/{specId}/{language}/{library}`, gave the schema a table of languages, and gave every
  plot a light and a dark render.
- Where: #5289, `app/src/pages/AboutPage.tsx`, `CHANGELOG.md`
