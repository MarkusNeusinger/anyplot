# The chat's short list of date forms takes four more that widely used tools write

- Date: 2026-11-19
- Proposed: a decision card with three options for the dates the list left unread: the two zone
  spellings (a time followed by ` UTC`, as BigQuery exports write it, and a UTC offset after a
  space, as git and Ruby write it); those two and a 12-hour clock with AM or PM, as US Excel writes
  it; or none of them, and let the count of refusals decide later.
- Decided: the two zone spellings and the 12-hour clock, and, on his own word in the chat, a fourth
  form: a date with the year first and slashes (`2026/03/01`). The reason is the card's, which he
  accepted: tools his audience uses every day write these forms, and none of them needs a guess.
