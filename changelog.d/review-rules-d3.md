### Changed

- **The review states four rules its judges already applied, and judges
  canvas use by eye.** An axis fits its data (zero only for bars, areas or a
  reference the reader needs), ticks let a reader read a value off (color bars
  included), every color, line style, marker or shading a reader must tell
  apart has a key or a direct label, and a label on a colored fill takes its
  color from that fill. The layout score no longer rates canvas use in
  percentages: the review asks whether the same drawing, enlarged, would still
  fit with its title, labels and legend. The generator prompts' axis-limit
  example, which started at zero, now says that zero is the baseline of a bar
  chart and that lines and points take their limits from the data.
