## A fifth rule for a user's plot

This rule joins the four above and, like them, takes precedence over the rubric.

5. **One data-derived highlight is part of a user's plot, not variant creep.** On a user's plot, at most one highlight that points the reader to a salient feature of the user's data (the maximum or minimum, a clear outlier, a peak, the latest value, the largest group), whose position and text the code computes from the data, is allowed on every spec, `-basic` ones included: do not deduct SC-01 for it, and credit it under DE-02 (visual hierarchy and storytelling) when it helps the reader see what matters. Everything else the rubric calls creep still is: a second highlight, a highlight at a literal value, a new encoding, a trend, mean or reference line, a band.
