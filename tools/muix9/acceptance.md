For a square spec the directive `//# anyplot-orientation: square` goes on line 5, directly below the header, not on line 1: the pipeline's header writer replaces the leading `//` block and stops at a `//#` line, and a directive on line 1 makes it prepend a second header (the stale-header defect you see on 131 of these files).
